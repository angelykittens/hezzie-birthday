To my Hezzie, with love 💌

Happy birthday, Hezzie. ♡ I hope this new chapter brings you so many reasons to smile, new things to be excited about, and little moments that remind you how loved you are. Makasih ya, for being you. For the laughs, the random conversations, and all the little things you might not even realize mean a lot to me. I hope you know that you deserve good things—not just today, but every day. I may not always find the perfect words, but I'm always rooting for you. So here's to another year of you being wonderfully, ridiculously you. Please keep smiling, keep dreaming, and don't forget to eat properly, okay? That's a birthday order. 😾 I hope this little website makes you smile, even just a little. I love you, birthday boy. Happy birthday. ♡
